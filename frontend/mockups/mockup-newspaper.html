Featured Project

AILA: An AI Tutor That Respects Academic Integrity

A LangGraph-powered learning assistant serving thousands of students at scale, designed to guide rather than give answers.

By Clay Gendron · Lead GenAI Engineer, SNHU

Building an AI tutor that actually helps students learn — without doing their work for them — is one of the hardest problems in educational technology. AILA tackles this head-on with LangGraph-based agent architectures that create conversational flows guiding students through problems rather than solving them directly.

The system can identify when a student is struggling and adjust its approach accordingly. It serves thousands of students across Southern New Hampshire University, built on Azure with Databricks integration for analytics and continuous improvement.

What makes AILA different isn't the technology stack — it's the philosophy. Every design decision starts with a question: does this help the student learn, or does it help the student avoid learning?
